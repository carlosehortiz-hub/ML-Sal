✅ EXECUTION ORDER FOR THE ML-Sal PROJECT

Step 0 — (optional, just to confirm)

base_dados/verificar_base.py
→ confirms that the database and table exist

⸻

Step 1 — Prepare the data

ml/preparar_dados_ml.py
→ cleans data, imputes, and creates the final ML dataset

⸻

Step 2 — Data quality report

utilitarios/qualidade_dados.py
→ reports missing values, rule violations, and IQR outliers

⸻

Step 3 — Train the model

ml/treinar_modelo_baseline.py
→ trains the Random Forest and evaluates performance (R², RMSE)

⸻

Step 4 — Global explanation

analise/explicacao_global.py
→ generates the global SHAP chart (outputs/shap_global.png)

⸻

Step 5 — Local explanation (specific case)

analise/explicacao_local.py
→ generates the local SHAP (outputs/shap_local.png) for a selected record

⸻

Step 6 — What-if simulations

analise/simulacao_what_if.py
→ simulates variable changes and observes the impact on predicted deviation

⸻

Whenever you add a new deviation to the database:

👉 restart at Step 1 and proceed as needed.
