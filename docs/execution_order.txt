ml/prepare_ml_data.py

ml/train_baseline_model.py

analysis/global_explanation.py

analysis/local_explanation.py

Step 6 — What-if simulations

analysis/what_if_simulation.py
→ simulates variable changes and observes the impact on predicted deviation
